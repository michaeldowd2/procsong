Procsong

An open-source schema for deterministic, clip-based, infinite music. A package of audio parts and a seed produce the same arrangement on any player: tracks of different lengths overlap and recombine, so the piece keeps going without a fixed loop.

  • Party backgrounds
  • Shops & cafes
  • Study music
  • Games
  • DJ compositions
  • Art installations
  • Practice companion
